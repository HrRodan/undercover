# UnderCover Travel Insurance: Policy Wording, short version

Amounts in USD. Policy record shows plan, add-ons, trip dates, trip cost and travelers.

## Limits

|Benefit|Economy|Premium|First|
|---|---|---|---|
|Cancellation and Interruption maximum|1,500|5,000|10,000|
|Trip Delay waiting period, hours|12|6|3|
|Trip Delay maximum|300|800|1,500|
|Baggage Delay waiting period, hours|24|12|6|
|Baggage Delay maximum|100|300|500|
|Baggage maximum|750|1,500|3,000|
|Per-item limit|150|300|500|
|Passport maximum|150|250|400|
|Emergency Medical maximum|25,000|100,000|250,000|
|Deductible|100|50|0|

The deductible ($100/$50/$0) applies once per claim to **Baggage** and **Emergency Medical** only ($0 for others).

## Cancellation and Interruption

Covered reasons ONLY (decline others):
1. Sickness, injury or death of traveler, companion or family member, confirmed in writing by a physician.
2. Severe weather or natural disaster stopping carrier 24+ hours.
3. Home or destination uninhabitable by **fire, flood or storm** only.
4. Jury duty or subpoena.
5. Job loss through no fault after 1+ year with employer.

Cancellation pays non-refundable prepaid costs. Interruption pays unused non-refundable prepaid costs plus a one-way economy fare home. Subtract refunds/credits first, then cap at the lower of trip cost or plan max ($0 deductible).

## Trip Delay

Carrier delays departure by at least the waiting period ONLY for: mechanical/crew problem, canceled service, severe weather or strike (decline other causes). Return delay cover continues until home.
Pays meals, accommodation, local transport and rebooking fees during delay, up to max ($0 deductible). Needs receipts and carrier confirmation of **both** delay and cause (if carrier document text is shown and states only times without the cause, refer).

## Baggage Delay

Checked baggage arrives at least the waiting period after arrival at a destination (not on journey home). Pays essential clothing and toiletries (all clothing and toiletries bought while waiting are covered), up to max ($0 deductible). Needs receipts and delayed baggage report (if receipt dates/locations disagree with the story, refer under Claims rather than declining).

## Baggage

Loss, theft or accidental damage during the trip, up to max after plan deductible (**$100 Economy, $50 Premium, $0 First**).
- **Per-item limit ($150/$300/$500):** Applies to each individual item (for a multi-unit line, divide line amount by quantity: if unit price `<= limit`, allow the **entire line amount** uncapped; only a pair or set, such as a pair of shoes, a suit or a camera with its lens, is combined and capped once).
- **Third-party payments:** Subtract carrier, hotel or third-party payments.
- **Documents (item receipts not required; pay all covered items):** Theft needs police report within 24h; carrier loss/damage needs carrier report; accidental damage needs repair estimate or unrepairable statement; other accidental loss needs incident note/letter.
- **Not covered (decline):** Unattended belongings in public, or in a vehicle outside a locked trunk/glove box out of sight; cash >$100; wear and tear; moths, vermin, insects or pets; value of data, writing or work (exclude data/writing/work items; if unattended, decline).

## Passport

Replacement fees, photos and local travel to embassy, up to max ($0 deductible). Needs receipts (story serves as account).

## Emergency Medical

Necessary treatment of sudden sickness or injury during trip, up to max after plan deductible (**$100 Economy, $50 Premium, $0 First**). Needs itemized bill/invoice and medical records.
Not covered (decline): pre-existing conditions (symptoms, physician visit, treatment or medication change in 60 days before purchase, including a chronic flare-up) unless **Pre-Existing Condition Waiver** applies; adventure sports injuries unless **Adventure Sports** applies; care that could wait until home.

## Add-ons

Apply ONLY when on the policy record:
- **Adventure Sports:** Adventure sports are ONLY bungee jumping, skydiving, paragliding, scuba below 10m, heli-skiing, roped mountaineering and white-water rafting above grade 3 (covered with this add-on; other activities covered without it; exclusion applies only to Emergency Medical, not Baggage).
- **Pre-Existing Condition Waiver:** Removes the 60-day pre-existing exclusion.
- **Electronics Plus:** **$1,200** per-item limit ONLY for **phones, tablets, laptops, cameras and their lenses (as one set), e-readers and smartwatches** (cap other items at $150/$300/$500).

## Never covered

Decline (never refer): incidents outside trip dates (except pre-departure Cancellation or return Trip Delay); events known before purchase; consequential losses such as lost earnings, missed events or penalties (exclude only the consequential item and pay remaining covered items); breaking the law or alcohol/drug impairment; war, unrest, nuclear; false statements or altered documents.

## Payment

`Payout = min(Benefit Max, (Sum of capped eligible items) - (Third-party payments) - (Deductible))`
1. Cap each covered item/set at the per-item limit and sum them (**do NOT cap at Benefit Max in Step 1**).
2. Subtract carrier or third-party payments/refunds/credits.
3. Subtract deductible (**$100 Economy, $50 Premium, $0 First**) once **ONLY for Baggage and Emergency Medical** ($0 for others).
4. Cap at Benefit Max.

## Claims

File within 90 days. Refer **ONLY** when a required document named above (or Trip Delay carrier cause confirmation) is missing, or when receipt dates/locations disagree with the story; otherwise approve or decline.
Family member: spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild. Physician: unrelated doctor. Traveling companion: co-traveler on policy.
