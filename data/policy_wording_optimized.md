# UnderCover Travel Insurance: Policy Wording

Version 2026.1, for policies bought on or after January 1, 2026. Your policy record shows your plan, add-ons, covered trip dates and travelers. All amounts are in US dollars.

The deductible ($100 Economy, $50 Premium, $0 First) applies once per claim ONLY to **Section 5 (Baggage)** and **Section 7 (Emergency Medical)** ($0 for all other benefits).

## 2. Trip Cancellation and Interruption

We pay ONLY when you cancel your trip before it starts, or cut it short, for one of these 5 reasons (decline any other reason):

1. Sickness, injury or death of you, a companion or a family member, confirmed in writing by a physician.
2. Severe weather or natural disaster stopping your carrier's service for 24+ hours.
3. Your home or destination made uninhabitable by **fire, flood or storm** only.
4. Jury duty or court subpoena.
5. Job loss through no fault of your own after 1+ year with the employer.

Cancellation pays non-refundable prepaid trip costs. Interruption pays unused non-refundable prepaid costs plus a one-way economy fare home. Subtract supplier refunds and credits first, then pay up to the lower of trip cost or your plan's maximum ($0 deductible).

## 3. Trip Delay

We pay when your carrier delays departure for at least your waiting period ONLY because of: a mechanical or crew problem, a canceled service, severe weather or a strike (decline other causes). If a return delay keeps you traveling past your return date, cover continues until you get home.

We pay reasonable meals, accommodation, local transport and rebooking fees during the delay, up to your plan's maximum ($0 deductible). Send us receipts and the carrier's confirmation of **both** the delay and its covered cause (if the carrier's document states only departure/arrival times and omits the cause, refer the claim).

## 4. Baggage Delay

We pay when your carrier delivers checked baggage at least your waiting period after you arrive at your destination (not on your journey home). We pay for essential clothing and toiletries bought while you wait (all clothing and toiletries bought during the delay are eligible), up to your plan's maximum ($0 deductible). Send us receipts and the carrier's delayed baggage report (if receipts and your story disagree on dates or location, always refer under Section 11 rather than declining).

## 5. Baggage and Personal Effects

We pay for loss, theft or accidental damage to your baggage and belongings during your covered trip, up to your plan's maximum after subtracting your plan's deductible ($100/$50/$0).

- **Per-item limit ($150/$300/$500):** Applies to every individual item (when a claim line lists a quantity of multiple separate items, divide line amount by quantity: if unit price `<= limit`, allow the **entire line amount** uncapped; if `> limit`, allow `quantity * limit`). Only a pair or set, such as a pair of shoes, a suit or a camera with its lens, counts as **one item** (even across separate lines: sum the pieces and cap once).
- **Third-party payments:** Anything a carrier, hotel or other party pays for the same loss is taken off.
- **Documents (item purchase receipts are not required; pay all covered items up to the per-item limit without requiring item receipts):**
  - Theft: police report made within 24 hours.
  - Carrier loss or damage: the carrier's report.
  - Accidental damage: repair estimate or statement that the item cannot be repaired.
  - Other accidental loss during the trip (not theft and not by a carrier): covered with an incident note or statement.

We do not pay for (decline):

- **Belongings left unattended:** Out of sight and reach in a public place, or in a vehicle unless locked in the trunk or glove box out of sight.
- **Cash above $100.**
- **Wear and tear, or gradual deterioration.**
- **Damage caused by moths, vermin, insects or pets.**
- **The value of information:** We pay for the physical item, not the data, writing or work it holds.

## 6. Passport and Travel Documents

If your passport or visa is lost, stolen, damaged or destroyed during your covered trip, we pay replacement fees, photos and local travel to the embassy or consulate, up to your plan's maximum ($0 deductible). Send us receipts and a short account.

## 7. Emergency Medical

We pay for necessary treatment of a sudden sickness or injury during your covered trip, up to your plan's maximum after subtracting your plan's deductible ($100/$50/$0). Send us the itemized bill/invoice and medical records.

We do not pay for (decline):

- **Pre-existing conditions:** Symptoms, physician visit, treatment or medication change in the 60 days before purchase, unless you bought **Pre-Existing Condition Waiver**.
- **Injuries from adventure sports**, unless you bought **Adventure Sports**.
- **Treatment that can reasonably wait until you are home.**

## 8. Add-ons

Apply an add-on ONLY when listed on your policy record:

**Adventure Sports.** Adventure sports are ONLY bungee jumping, skydiving, paragliding, scuba diving deeper than 10 meters (33 feet), heli-skiing, roped mountaineering and white-water rafting above grade 3 (other activities are covered without this add-on, and this exclusion applies only to Section 7 medical injuries, never Section 5 baggage). Covers injuries from these 7 sports with required certification and within limits.

**Pre-Existing Condition Waiver.** Removes the 60-day pre-existing condition exclusion in Section 7 completely (even with symptoms, physician visit, treatment or medication change in the 60 days before purchase).

**Electronics Plus.** Raises the per-item limit to **$1,200 ONLY for these 6 items: phones, tablets, laptops, cameras and their lenses, e-readers and smartwatches** (other items use your plan's standard limit).

## 9. General exclusions

No benefit pays for (always decline, never refer):

- Incidents outside your covered trip dates (except pre-departure Cancellation or covered return Trip Delay).
- Events publicly known before purchase, such as a named storm or announced strike.
- Consequential losses, such as lost earnings, missed events or penalties (exclude only the consequential item and pay remaining covered items).
- Losses or injuries while breaking the law or impaired by alcohol or drugs.
- War, civil unrest and nuclear incidents.
- False statements or altered documents (when the story and attached documents disagree, refer under Section 11).

## 10. How we work out your payment

`Payout = min(Benefit Maximum, (Sum of capped eligible items) - (Third-party payments) - (Deductible))`

1. **Eligible items:** Cap each covered individual item or combined pair/set at the per-item limit and sum them (**never cap at the benefit maximum in Step 1**).
2. **Third-party payments:** Take off anything a carrier, supplier or other party paid, refunded or credited.
3. **Deductible:** Take off the deductible ($100/$50/$0) once per claim **ONLY for Section 5 (Baggage) and Section 7 (Emergency Medical)** ($0 for all other sections).
4. **Benefit maximum:** Pay the Step 3 result, up to the benefit maximum (or lower of trip cost and plan maximum for Section 2).

## 11. How to claim

File within 90 days of the incident.
- **Refer (`payout: 0`):** ONLY when (a) a required document named in the section (or Section 3 carrier cause confirmation) is missing, OR (b) the story and attached documents disagree (always refer rather than declining when receipt dates/locations disagree with the story). Never refer when required documents are present—the wording above settles all coverage questions.
- **Decline (`payout: 0`):** When an exclusion applies or the waiting period/covered reason is not met.

## 12. Definitions

- **Carrier:** airline, railway, ferry, cruise or coach company you hold a ticket with.
- **Covered trip:** the trip on your policy record, from departure date to return date.
- **Family member:** spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or grandchild.
- **Physician:** licensed doctor who is not you, a companion or a family member.
- **Traveling companion:** a person named as a traveler on your policy record.
